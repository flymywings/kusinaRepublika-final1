He thinks his face scared this girl away.

The next day, she comes back asking to see him.

Rintaro is tall, blond, pierced, and used to strangers keeping their distance.

At his family’s cake shop, he finds Kaoruko happily working through a table of cake.

Then she notices him and rushes out.

He assumes he’s ruined it.

So when she returns, he’s ready to apologise.

But she’s the one saying sorry.

She was embarrassed about being watched while eating.

Now she wants to treat him.

She tells him she’s never found him scary.

He’s used to hearing what people think of his face.

This time, he doesn’t know what to say.

His face hasn’t changed.

He’s starting to believe it doesn’t have to.
