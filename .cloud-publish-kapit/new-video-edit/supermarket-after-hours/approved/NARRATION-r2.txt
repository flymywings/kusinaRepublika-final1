He’s denying a crush on his favourite cashier.

He hasn’t realised he’s talking to her.

After another exhausting shift, forty-five-year-old Sasaki stops at the supermarket.

Yamada’s smile usually makes the trip worth it.

Tonight, someone else is at her register.

Outside, a woman asks him for a light.

Different clothes. Piercings. A teasing smile.

She brings up Yamada.

He gets flustered. It isn’t like that.

He just looks forward to those few seconds when someone seems happy to see him.

Her teasing stops.

He thinks she’s looking out for her coworker.

She introduces herself as Tayama.

Now he has someone to talk to about Yamada.

He came to the store hoping someone would brighten his day.

He just brightened hers.
