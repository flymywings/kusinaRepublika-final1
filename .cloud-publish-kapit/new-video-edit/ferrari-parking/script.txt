A billionaire borrowed ONE dollar and handed the bank the keys to his Ferrari. He walked into a Manhattan bank, requested a one-dollar loan, and watched guards drive his half-million-dollar supercar into their underground vault. Two weeks later, he returned, paid back the dollar, and gave the teller two cents in interest. The banker asked why a wealthy man needed a one-dollar loan. The billionaire smiled: where else in Manhattan can you park a Ferrari for two weeks for two cents?
