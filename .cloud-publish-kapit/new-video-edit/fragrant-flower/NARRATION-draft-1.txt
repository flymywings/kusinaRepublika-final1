He thinks this girl ran away because he looks scary.
She’s back to buy him cake.

Rintaro is tall, blond, and used to strangers keeping their distance.

When Kaoruko rushes out of his family’s bakery after seeing him, he assumes he’s frightened another customer.

But she comes back to apologise.

She was embarrassed about being watched while eating.

Then she tells him she’s never found him scary.

For once, he lets himself smile.

At school, he pulls back a classroom curtain.

She’s in the building opposite.

Kaoruko goes to the girls’ school that looks down on boys from his.

He thought someone like her was a world away.

Now he has a reason to open the curtains.
