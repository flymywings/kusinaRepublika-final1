Two men struck it rich by putting diamonds into the ground.
In 1872, they led investors to a remote spot in Colorado. Diamonds! Rubies! Visitors picked them right off the ground. Even a mining engineer approved it. The men walked away with hundreds of thousands of dollars.
But investigators noticed something odd: gems appeared only where someone had disturbed the ground.
The men had stocked the site with stones bought in London.
They weren’t mining diamonds. They were mining investors.
