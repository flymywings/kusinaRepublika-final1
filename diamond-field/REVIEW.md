## Reusable lessons from this build

- Lock character marks, hand targets, gem identities, and transfer timing before parallel production.
- Check drawing order as well as 3D coordinates: the hand lens was initially hidden by the head fill.
- Avoid steep overhead views of vertical character cards when their bodies remain in frame.
- Use camera framing to compare evidence without losing the observer's attention and gesture.
- Keep the final consequence large enough to read; the ending favors the investors' faces.
