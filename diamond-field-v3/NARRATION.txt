Two men sold a fake diamond mine, but the diamonds were real.

Colorado, eighteen seventy-two.

Investors picked diamonds and rubies straight off the ground.

They brought their own mining expert. He approved the site.

So they paid hundreds of thousands. The two men cashed out.

But investigators found gems only in disturbed soil.

They dug deeper. Nothing.

The sellers had bought the stones in London and planted them.

They weren’t mining diamonds.

They were mining investors.
