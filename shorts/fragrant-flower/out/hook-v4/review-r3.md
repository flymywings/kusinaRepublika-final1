**8/10, my opinion.** The mirror and return visit now read at phone size. The one major still open is the second coin release. The three things keeping the opening below the best references are the fleeting evidence of that payment, the mildness of her startle, and the amount of attention the orange tabletop takes from her face.

## First read at 360 px

I saw a girl with her fork raised, a blond man above her in a mirror, and a pile of desserts hiding her mouth. The backwards title on the menu registered immediately. The dark arc at her left read as the back of her head once I saw the lettering. On its own the arc could be the booth. His heavy lids and still mouth give her wariness a target. She looks caught or apprehensive. “Terrified” is a stronger emotion than I got from her eyes.

By about four seconds I read a second visit. The plates clear, the orange light goes away and the pink school sleeve comes forward to pay. My question was **“What made her come back and buy him cake if she was so wary of him?”** I saw f0 and the sequence before reading the scene card. The assignment itself named the mirror cues, and the sequence had captions. This is a reviewer's read. The once-through, unbriefed audience test with sound is still open.
