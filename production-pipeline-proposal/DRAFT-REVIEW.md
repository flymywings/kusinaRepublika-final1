## The substance

> He does to her the exact thing everyone does to him: he looks at a face and decides what it means.

People read his face as danger. He reads hers as fear. Both are wrong.
Both characters dread the same thing, being looked at. The ending is two people who can stand being looked at by each other.

Three things carry it without a word of new narration:
1. **The look.** A matched point-of-view shot used three times: strangers at him, him at her, her at him.
2. **His size.** He keeps folding himself smaller. At the end he stops.
3. **The cake.** Evidence of her shame, then her apology, then something they share.
