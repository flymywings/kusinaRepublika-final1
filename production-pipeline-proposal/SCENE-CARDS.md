**The idea:** he does to her what everyone does to him. He looks at a face and decides what it means.

**How we show it:** his misreading is cool and melts everything but her eyes. The truth is warm and opens the focus.

**The planted clue:** on the first frame her fork ticks on the plate and it looks like fear. At the turn, the same tick at the same rhythm is her heart.

**The mirror:** an old mirror behind her booth puts her face and his reflected face in one picture. It opens the film, the rewind returns to it, and it ends the film.

## Decisions

1. **How shots change.** A: most changes are camera moves, focus pulls, matched dissolves or masked whips, with a hard cut only where those don't fit, and every change on a word. B: hard cuts every 1.5-2 s. **Recommend A.** It keeps your pace and the 20 references' rule that hard cuts are rare. Their median shot is 2.9 s, so 1.5-2 s puts us at their fast end, which 3 of the 20 reach.
2. **Sign and menu text.** The cafe sign says "Patisserie Tsumugi" and the menu lists real cake names. A: replace all of it with invented words, in the new copy of the set only. B: change only the sign name. **Recommend A.** It follows "made-up words only" literally.
3. **Ending.** A: the hook framing again, with her seated and his reflection staying in the mirror, both faces in one picture. B: a real two-shot of them at the table. **Recommend A.** Both are in frame, and the last image answers the first.

Earlier picks still stand: Hook B, cool reading to warm truth, narration v3. Approving also accepts the staging and exceptions below.
